PLAY TOGETHER
Two players on the same network, signed in to the same Jellyfin. Whoever joins comes in as a customer: any tape's free, and nobody throws them out at closing. You'll see each other and share the time and weather (either of you can change them: hold F). Everything else is your own copy of the store, so customers and tapes won't match.
